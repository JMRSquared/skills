# Product

## What           Bike repairs in Woodstock (a fictional shop, used only for this demo)
## For            Cyclists nearby with a broken bike, usually on a phone
## Action         Book on WhatsApp
## Facts          82 Albert Road, Woodstock. Tuesday to Saturday. Drop off by 10, collect the same day. Puncture fix R150, full service R950, wheel truing R220
## Objects        1. A workshop wall you pull tools from. 2. A bike you take apart by dragging. 3. A repair ticket on a clip. 4. A chain you pull link by link. 5. A wheel you flick. Chose the wheel: everyone knows how a bike wheel spins, its inertia is satisfying and each slow-down lands on a repair and its price, so it replaces the price section's first read
## Assets         Procedural three.js wheel in a truing stand: chrome spokes, gumwall tyre, tread, cassette. ACES tone mapping, real soft shadows. Unsplash photographs for the rest
## Story          Scroll holds the wheel and turns it through all three repairs, then hands over to the pointer. Then one full-bleed workshop photo, the turnaround, prices beside a photo, the address
