Detent 01 of 24

The knob clicks through 24 detents in one full turn

Each detent is a ball bearing dropping into a milled pocket. You feel it in your fingertips before you hear it.

Size, bore, finish and price of the Type 4 Rotary

Every feature on the knob has a job. The knurl is there because fingers slip on anodised aluminium. The flat in the bore is there because a grub screw on a round shaft works loose by the fourth month.
