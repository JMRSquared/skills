Bread baked from flour we mill in Margate

We have milled Maris Widgeon and Yeoman wheat on a granite stone at the back of the shop since 2016. Every loaf ferments for twenty-eight hours and bakes in a deck oven lit every morning since 1974.

We sell bread from 07:30 until it runs out, which on a Saturday is usually before noon. We do not hold any back, freeze it or buy it in.

We mill our flour every Tuesday and Friday

Everything we bake in the next forty-eight hours comes from that milling. That is why a loaf you buy on a Sunday tastes different from one you bought on a Wednesday.
