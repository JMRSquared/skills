# Product
## Register
brand

## Users
Woodstock and city-bowl commuters, students and weekend riders standing next to a bike with a flat tyre. In five seconds they need: do you fix bikes near me, can I have it back today, how much, how do I book.

## Purpose
Get the visitor to send a WhatsApp message to book a repair. Second job: get locals in on a Saturday morning for coffee.

## Personality
Warm, cheeky, handy

## Anti-references
- No chrome-and-carbon "performance cycling" look: black backgrounds, italic sport type, lycra hero photos.
- No hipster third-wave coffee template: beige minimalism, thin serif, a latte-art stock photo.
- No hero plus three icon-in-a-box feature cards, no AI gradient blobs, no stock mechanic with a spanner.
- No booking form or calendar widget. Booking is a WhatsApp chat, and the page shows that chat.

## Principles
1. Prices in rands on the page, next to the repair they belong to.
2. Same-day punctures are the headline promise: in by 10:00, out after lunch.
3. The cheek lives in the details (stickers, side notes, the kettle's face); headings stay plain.
4. Every section is part of one workshop on Albert Road on a Saturday morning.
5. Every button opens WhatsApp or jumps to prices; nothing else asks for a click.
