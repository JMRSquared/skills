A wood-fired bakery under the arches

Seven things, baked daily.

One oven, one firing, one list. When the shelf is empty we shut the door and start the next day’s levain.

Lamination bench, 04.10. The oven is lit at 03.00 and holds heat until two in the afternoon.

Today at the counter

Prices are per loaf or per piece. Everything is made with Shipton Mill flour, water, salt and time; the levain is fourteen years old.
