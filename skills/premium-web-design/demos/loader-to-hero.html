Behind the bar

Six whiskies, chosen by Joost

The cocktail list is twelve deep and changes when the fruit changes. Wine is mostly Loire and Jura, mostly under €50 a bottle. The whisky shelf is short on purpose, and Joost will talk you through all six if you let him.

Kitchen

Six plates, written on Tuesday

Sofie cooks alone on a two-burner induction and a plancha. The menu is whatever the Wednesday market gave her, which is why it is written by hand and why it runs out.
