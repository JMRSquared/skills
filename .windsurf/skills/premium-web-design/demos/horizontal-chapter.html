Malt whisky distilled in Grindale since 1968

The distillery is four rooms in a row, from the mash house to the warehouse. A tour takes you through them in the order the barley moves.

We mash ten tonnes of Yorkshire barley a week

We mash it at 64°C in a cast-iron tun that has stood in the same spot since 1968. Three waters run through each batch, and the third one starts the next batch.

Mash house: 64°C, 10 tonnes a week

One person watches every run through our two stills

The wash still holds 4,500 litres and runs for eight hours. The spirit still holds 2,700 litres, and one person watches it for the whole run. Nobody has found a meter that beats a nose.

Still room: 4,500 and 2,700 litres, eight-hour runs

We weigh every cask before and after we fill it

Casks arrive by the lorry-load and leave one at a time. The ten-year-old goes into first-fill bourbon casks, and the rest goes into refill sherry casks.

Filling store: 200-litre casks, first fill for the ten-year-old

Our whisky ages in 1,140 casks in Warehouse No. 3

The casks sit three high on an earth floor, with no heating and no racks. About 2.1% of the spirit escapes through the roof each year. That slow loss is why a nine-year-old tastes different from a three-year-old.

Warehouse No. 3: 1,140 casks, 2.1% lost each year
