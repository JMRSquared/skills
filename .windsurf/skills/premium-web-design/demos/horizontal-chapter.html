Chapter 01

Mash house

Ten tonnes of Yorkshire barley a week, mashed at 64°C in a cast-iron tun that has not moved since 1968. Three waters, and the third one starts the next batch.

64°C · 10 t / week

Chapter 02

Still room

Two stills, 4,500 and 2,700 litres. The wash still runs eight hours. The spirit still is watched by one person for the whole of it, because nobody has found a meter that beats a nose.

4,500 L · 8 hours

Chapter 03

Filling store

Casks arrive by the lorry-load and leave one at a time. First-fill bourbon for the ten year old, refill sherry for the rest. Every one is weighed empty and weighed again full.

200 L · first fill

Chapter 04

Warehouse No. 3

Dunnage floor, casks three high, no heating and no racking. It gives 2.1% a year back to the roof. That loss is not waste, it is the reason the ninth year tastes different from the third.

2.1% / year · 1,140 casks
