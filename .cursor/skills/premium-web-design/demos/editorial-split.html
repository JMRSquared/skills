The practice

Eleven people, six buildings a year.

Ida Halden and Samuel Roe started the office in 2009 above a joinery in Nørrebro. We take on work we can reach by train, we draw our own details, and we put one of the eleven on every site every week.

The three below are finished and occupied. Two more are on site in Suffolk and Malmö.

Vestergade Waterworks

Copenhagen · 2023 · 2,140 m²

A pumping station of 1908, emptied in 1974 and left open to the weather for a decade. The original brick vaults now carry a new cast floor, forty desks and a canteen the neighbourhood can walk into. Nothing above the cill line was demolished.

Lower vault, looking east toward the old inlet.

Kilnhouse

Blythburgh, Suffolk · 2024 · 186 m²

A house and a working kiln for a potter, set into the fall of the land so the studio sits half a storey below the living floor. The roof-lights are cast into the slab rather than cut through it, which holds even north light from ten until four.

Studio ceiling. 214 details were drawn at 1:5; the contractor raised no query on the envelope.

Nordre Gymnasium

Aarhus · 2022 · 980 m²

An assembly hall for four hundred, built inside the footprint of the 1971 sports block it replaced. The downstand beams are the acoustic treatment — there is no applied absorption anywhere in the room, and speech carries to the back row unamplified.

Hall at midday, north gallery.

Method

The structure is the first drawing and the last.

We do not design a shape and then ask an engineer to hold it up. Three habits carry most of what the office is; none of them is a piece of software.

01

Structure first

Every project opens with a structural sketch at 1:200 and a written load path on one side of A4. If the diagram needs a paragraph of explanation, it is the wrong diagram and we start again.

02

Detailed in-house

We draw our own junctions rather than buying a system. It is slower in the office and faster on site: Kilnhouse ran fourteen weeks of drawing and zero envelope queries during construction.

03

On site every week

One of the eleven walks each site every week from groundworks to handover, with a camera and no agenda. It is the only quality system we have found that survives contact with a wet January.
