# Product
## Register
brand

## Users
Woodstock and city-bowl commuters, students and weekend riders standing next to a bike with a flat tyre. In five seconds they need: do you fix bikes near me, can I have it back today, how much, how do I book.

## Purpose
Get the visitor to send a WhatsApp message to book a repair. Second job: get locals in on a Saturday morning for coffee.

## Personality
Warm, cheeky, handy

## Anti-references
- No chrome-and-carbon "performance cycling" look: black backgrounds, italic sport type, lycra hero photos.
- No hero plus three icon-in-a-box feature cards, no AI gradient blobs, no stock mechanic with a spanner.
- No booking form or calendar widget. Booking is a WhatsApp chat, and the page shows that chat.
- No section that only fades its content in. Every scene changes the bike or the chat.

## Principles
1. Prices in rands on the page, next to the repair they belong to.
2. Same-day punctures are the headline promise: in by 10:00, out after lunch.
3. The cheek lives in the details (stickers, side notes, the kettle's face); headings stay plain.
4. Every button opens WhatsApp or jumps to prices; nothing else asks for a click.

## Story
Protagonist  The red bike with the teal kettle in its basket, booked in as ticket #0417 with a flat rear tyre
Change       Flat at 09:12, ridden home at 13:05. The page is that one morning on the workstand
Spine        Ticket #0417, pinned beside the bike, stamps one line per chapter: in, patched, trued, ready
Peak         "The front wheel spun straight while I scrolled and the wobble counted down to zero"
Ending       The bike rides out of frame and leaves the booking button behind

## Feeling curve
1  Recognition   a bike with a flat tyre, which is why they are here
2  Relief        it goes up on the stand and the tyre fills
3  Delight       the wobble readout counts to zero while the wheel spins (peak)
4  Confidence    back on the floor with a time on the ticket
5  Ease          booking is three messages they can read
6  Resolve       the bike rides off; one button is left
