Kiln & Rye

Arch 118, Druid Street, SE1

Open Wednesday to Sunday from 7.30

Wood-fired bread and pastry in Bermondsey

We bake seven breads and pastries a day in one oven under the Druid Street arches. When the shelf is empty we shut the door and start the next day’s levain.

The lamination bench at 04.10. We light the oven at 03.00 and it holds its heat until 2pm.

The seven breads and pastries we bake each day, with prices

Prices are per loaf or per piece. We make everything with Shipton Mill flour, and our levain is fourteen years old.

    £5.20

    Seeded spelt bâtard

    Spelt with sunflower seeds, linseed and a soaker made from last week’s crusts.

    £5.60
  • 04

    Walnut and raisin

    Kent walnuts, soaked flame raisins and a little honey. We bake it to eat with cheese.

    £6.40
  • 05

    Cardamom bun

    A knotted bun with pearl sugar and cardamom we grind the same morning.

    £4.10
  • 06

    Butter croissant

    We laminate the dough over three days with 28% Jersey butter. Croissants come out at 08.00 only.

    £3.60
  • 07

    Pain au chocolat

    Two batons of Valrhona 64% chocolate in each. We make thirty-two a day.

    £4.20

We supply restaurants and cafés within three miles of the arches. Order by Thursday and we deliver by bicycle before 6am.

Come to Arch 118 on Druid Street before nine for croissants

We sell the last croissant by half past nine, and on a Saturday most of the rye goes too. There are four stools and a hot plate, and we pour Assembly coffee, black or with milk.
