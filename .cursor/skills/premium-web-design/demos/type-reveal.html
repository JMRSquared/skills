Stone-ground since 2016

Margate, Kent

Flour. Water. Time.

We mill Maris Widgeon and Yeoman wheat on a granite stone at the back of the shop, ferment for twenty-eight hours, and bake in a single deck oven that has been lit every morning since 1974.

Bread is sold from 07:30 until it runs out, which on a Saturday is usually before noon. Nothing is held back, frozen or brought in.
